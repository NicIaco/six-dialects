Six Dialects · AI cinema

Read your film
in six grammars

Describe a film made with AI, or a tool used to make one. A film travels through many countries before it reaches its audience, and six capitals that govern AI each read it from a different place. This page reads your film the way each of them would.

For every grammar you get what it takes for granted about your film and what in it would read as wrong, then the decision that follows. After the six readings come the forks, where two grammars contradict each other about your film. Last comes the moment a person stops weighing the answer for themselves.

Describing a grammar is not endorsing it.

A grammar can be described accurately and still be wrong. Nothing here turns a rights violation into a dialect, and nothing here tells you which grammar to pick. That choice stays with you.

It gives you a reading, not a verdict.

02

Your Public AI key

The reading runs on Apertus, the open Swiss model, through Public AI. You bring your own key. It takes a minute to get one at platform.publicai.co, and a new account comes with free starter credit.

Your key goes to Public AI and nowhere else. On the way it passes through eofe.ai, because Public AI does not accept calls straight from a browser. There it is never stored or logged. If you tick the box, the key stays saved in this browser only.
