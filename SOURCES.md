# Sources

Each dialect in `dialects.yaml` is a reading of real documents, not an
impression. This is where the reading comes from.

Links, not copies. These texts belong to the bodies that wrote them, and they
change; a snapshot in a repo goes stale and misrepresents them.

---

## Brussels · rights

- **EU AI Act** — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj
- **Ethics Guidelines for Trustworthy AI** — High-Level Expert Group on AI, 2019
  https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/library/ethics-guidelines-trustworthy-ai
- **GDPR** — Regulation (EU) 2016/679
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2016/679/oj
- **Charter of Fundamental Rights of the European Union**
  https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX:12012P/TXT

*Read for: where an argument starts when it starts from the person affected.*

## Washington · innovation and liberty

- **NIST AI Risk Management Framework 1.0**, 2023
  https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework

*Read for: liberty, contract, disclosure, and the assumption that the choice
belongs to the individual.*

## Beijing · harmony

- **Interim Measures for the Management of Generative AI Services**, CAC, 2023
  https://www.cac.gov.cn/2023-07/13/c_1690898327029107.htm
- **New Generation AI Development Plan**, State Council, 2017
- **Global AI Governance Initiative**, 2023

*Read for: stability, the collective, and responsibility running from the
person toward the group rather than the other way.*

## London · existential risk

- **The Bletchley Declaration**, AI Safety Summit, November 2023
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/ai-safety-summit-2023-the-bletchley-declaration
- **UK AI Security Institute** — publications and evaluations
  https://www.aisi.gov.uk/

*Read for: worst case, at scale, and irreversibility.*

## Singapore · compliance

- **Model AI Governance Framework**, IMDA / PDPC
  https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/help-and-resources/2020/01/model-ai-governance-framework
- **AI Verify** — testing framework and toolkit
  https://aiverifyfoundation.sg/

*Read for: what turns "responsible" into "conforming".*

## Rome · dignity

- **Rome Call for AI Ethics**, 2020
  https://www.romecall.org/
- **Rerum Novarum**, Leo XIII, 1891 — the precedent the recent texts claim

*Read for: the dignity of the person as prior to any rule written about them.*

## On the seventh place

A seventh position, Abu Dhabi, speaks capital rather than a moral grammar. It
is described in the Apolitical piece and deliberately excluded from the
scorer: no assistant answers a question about a dying parent in the language
of capital. If you think that is wrong, the argument belongs in an issue.
